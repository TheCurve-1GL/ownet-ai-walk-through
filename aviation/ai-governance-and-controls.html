How do controls scale with what the AI does?Read more

An AI that drafts for a person to check needs light rules. One that acts on its own needs testing before it goes live, monitoring, a named owner and a tested way to stop it.

Will this slow AI down?Read more

It shouldn't. Low-risk uses get light-touch rules. Tighter controls are kept for where something serious could go wrong.

Can you work with our risk team?Read more

Yes. We build AI into the framework your risk and audit teams already run, so there's one way of managing risk, not two.
