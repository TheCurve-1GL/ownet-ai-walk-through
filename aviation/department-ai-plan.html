Risk avoided, an indication

About a month if profiled evenly, though real incidents don't arrive evenly. It's shown on its own and never added to the total, because it depends on things that may not happen.

The planning figure is the value at full run. Gains build over three to four years: plan on about 15% in year one, 45% in year two and 75% in year three, as teams test, trust and adopt each change. "Bankable now" counts lower headcount in full and other gains at half until they are measured. Freed time is capacity, not cash: it becomes money only through lower headcount over time, slower hiring or less overtime, and the rest is people moved onto higher-value work. Every figure here is an illustrative estimate based on an airport with £1bn annual turnover and the assumptions on this page, not a benchmark or a promise of results.
