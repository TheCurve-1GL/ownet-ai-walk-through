The problem

You can't improve what you can't see

Most AI projects begin with a guess about how the work is done. Process maps, if they exist, are out of date, and the real process lives in people's heads and inboxes. That's why AI often lands in the wrong place, or automates a step that shouldn't exist at all.

A clear, measured model of the process fixes that. It shows where time goes, where errors creep in and which tasks are ready for AI, and it gives you a baseline to measure the change against.

This is for you if

  • Your process maps are missing or out of date
  • You want to know where AI fits before you buy tools
  • You need a baseline to prove what AI has saved
  • You're building towards a digital twin of your organisation
What we do

From real activity to a model you can use

Automated process modelling

We build process models from system data and short conversations, rather than weeks of workshops, so the picture is accurate and quick to produce.

Task-level detail

Each process is broken into tasks with times, volumes and handoffs, ready to sort into the four AI types.

Performance baseline

We capture how long things take and where errors happen today, so you can measure the change AI makes.

Built on ARIS

Ownet's Owners have used the ARIS platform for over 15 years. We export your models so you can reuse them, or once you see the value we can talk about whether ARIS is a good choice for you.

Controls and risk

Controls and risks are linked to the steps they protect, which helps with audit, resilience and AI governance.

Towards a digital twin

Over time the models join up into a digital twin of your organisation, so you can test what-if scenarios before you change anything.

How we sort the work

Every task goes into one of four types

Once the process is modelled, every task can be sorted. This is what turns a process map into an AI plan.

1

Keep it human

Judgement, relationships and decisions that need a person. AI stays out.

2

AI drafts, a person checks

AI does the first pass. A person reviews it and owns the result.

3

AI handles it

Routine, rules-based work that AI runs, with a person picking up the exceptions.

4

New with AI

Work you couldn't afford to do before, plus the oversight that keeps AI honest.

How it works

Four steps to an AI-ready model

Step 1

Scope

Pick the process and agree what good looks like with the people who own it.

Step 2

Capture

Gather system data and talk to the people who do the work to build the model quickly.

Step 3

Measure

Add times, volumes, costs, risks and controls to set a baseline.

Step 4

Hand over

Hand over the models, mark where AI could fit and keep them up to date as your processes change.

Questions

Straight answers

How is this different from an AI Process Deep Dive?

Process Modelling builds and maintains the models across your organisation, for AI, controls, audit and change. A Deep Dive uses that kind of model to analyse one process for AI in depth and plan the changes. Many clients start with modelling and add Deep Dives as they go.

Do we need ARIS to work with you?

No. We can host and share the models with you, or export them into the tools you already use.

How is this different from process mapping workshops?

Workshops capture what people think happens. Automated process modelling starts from what systems show actually happens, then uses short conversations to fill the gaps. It's quicker, more accurate and far less disruptive.

Can we reuse the model later?

Yes. That's the point. The same model supports AI, controls, resilience and future change, so the effort pays back many times.
