How we sort the work

Every task goes into one of four types

Every regular task in a process goes into one of four types. It's simple enough for the people doing the work to agree with, and precise enough for finance to check.

1

Keep it human

Judgement, relationships and decisions that need a person. AI stays out.

2

AI drafts, a person checks

AI does the first pass. A person reviews it and owns the result.

3

AI handles it

Routine, rules-based work that AI runs, with a person picking up the exceptions.

4

New with AI

Work you couldn't afford to do before, plus the oversight that keeps AI honest.

How we work

From first look to lasting change

Questions we're often asked

Straight answers

We already have Copilot. Isn't that enough?

It's a good head start. Tools are only half of it. We look at the work itself, process by process, so the licences you already pay for actually take time off people's desks.

Does this mean job cuts?

That isn't the aim. The work is designed to move people onto higher-value tasks, slow hiring or cut overtime. What you do with the freed time is your decision.

Where should we start?

Usually with one back-office process that has lots of routine, rules-based work, such as finance, procurement or HR. Those show the biggest share of time freed and are the easiest to measure.

Can this go on our existing Ownet order?

Yes. If you already work with Ownet, the work can usually go on your current order, so there's no new supplier to set up.
